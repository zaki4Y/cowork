# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Remote workers and freelancers looking for flexible coworking spaces. They're
professionals who need on-demand or monthly workspace — productive environments
without long-term leases. Their context is choosing and booking a space that fits
their work style, then managing their membership.

## Product Purpose

AURELIA connects remote professionals with premium coworking spaces. It
helps users discover, book, and manage workspace — from hot desks to private
offices — with transparent pricing and real-time availability. Success is a
seamless booking experience that feels as polished as the physical spaces.

## Brand Personality

Professional, warm, trustworthy. The brand evokes the feeling of walking into a
well-designed workspace — inviting yet productive, premium without pretension.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS corporate (gray boxes, stock photos, clip-art icons)
- Dark-themed "cool startup" patterns (neon accents, glassmorphism, gradients)
- Co-working clichés (exposed brick filters, ping-pong tables, bean bags)

## Design Principles

1. **Confidence through clarity** — Every page answers "what's here, what do I
   do?" in under 2 seconds. No decorative friction.
2. **Warm professionalism** — Inviting like a well-designed lobby, not cold like
   an airport lounge. The digital space should feel as good as the physical one.
3. **Space as hero** — Photography and imagery of the actual spaces drive the
   experience. UI steps back to let the environments speak.
4. **Trust through transparency** — Pricing, availability, and policies are
   forward, not buried. No surprise fees, no hidden login requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Consider reduced motion for scroll animations (GSAP/Framer Motion)
- Color contrast sufficient over the warm neutral palette
